# Nibble artwork

Created with the built-in image generation tool, using the user's approved Nibble sheets as art direction. Nothing is generated at runtime. The app loads `desk.png` and `character-atlas.png` once into cached premultiplied-alpha bitmaps at the display scale.

`character-atlas.png` is four columns by two rows: happy, breathe, blink, attentive; worried, critical, reset, resting. `desk.png` is the static environment. Native code draws all usage labels, bars, values, countdowns and status separately.

## Desk prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit target: the generated Nibble atlas reference. Produce ONE static environment asset only, NOT a sprite sheet, on genuine transparent background. Remove every slime character and every star completely. Recreate one approved rounded warm wooden miniature desk with dark short legs, a tiny black monitor at left front with mint slime symbol (no letters), compact white-key keyboard in middle, dark mouse at right, and dark chair behind right side. Same polished 3D cartoon materials and camera as reference. It is an EMPTY workspace awaiting the character sprite. Canvas square 1024x1024. Desk occupies x=45..980 y=580..930, monitor x=190..460 y=460..720, keyboard x=420..770 y=650..740, mouse x=800..940 y=670..780. Chair behind desk at right x=730..945 y=360..740. All the region above desk except chair is completely transparent, no cyan background, no holographic panel, no slime, no text, no other objects, no baked shadows outside objects, no labels. All objects fully contained with transparent margins. Single scene only, not grid. This static PNG will sit behind separate animated Nibble sprites and native dynamic HUD.

## Character atlas prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit. Input: approved Nibble scene atlas as CHARACTER IDENTITY REFERENCE. Make ONE production SPRITE ATLAS of the SLIME CHARACTER ONLY, remove EVERY desk, keyboard, mouse, monitor, chair, background and HUD. Genuine alpha transparent PNG. Exactly FOUR columns by TWO rows, eight EQUAL SQUARE cells, canvas 2048x1024, each cell512x512. No borders, captions, text, numbers, percentages, scenery or background haze. Preserve approved glossy translucent mint-green jelly body, large emerald eyes, pink cheeks, small expressive mouth, single jelly crest and small jelly hands, modern polished 3D cartoon look. Consistent front three-quarter camera as reference, and constant size across all eight cells. All character bases/hands anchored at x256 y450 in their cells; body width350px, head approx x90..430 y160..440, jelly crest may extend to y70. Do not randomly move or rescale between cells. Transparent margins 35px minimum. Each cell includes complete character, never clipped or crossing another cell. Top row left-to-right: happy idle open eyes joyful small mouth; same happy idle with gentle breathing lifted 3px; same with blink eyes closed mouth gentle smile; moderate attentive neutral open eyes small neutral mouth. Bottom row left-to-right: low worried tilted eyebrows small sad mouth drooped body; critical distressed worried eyes concerned open mouth body slightly lower; reset celebrating happy closed eyes joyful mouth hands slightly raised, tiny nearby cyan/gold stars only; sleeping peacefully closed eyes mouth tiny resting smile body drooped, no Z letters. No extra unrelated characters. No continuous glow around rectangular cells; only restrained highlights on slime itself. Match cells exactly for stable animation. This atlas is decoded once and drawn with alpha by a tiny native Windows app. NO desk or anything except the character and the few celebration stars.
